MovAi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

MovAi ("we", "us", "our") provides an AI content generation website where users create images, videos, 3D models and songs. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, to create and identify your MovAi account.
  • Content you create: prompts, titles, lyrics, styles and the images, videos, 3D models and songs you generate, when you choose to save or publish them.
  • Account and purchase information: your credit balances, package purchases and purchase requests, so we can provide the service and grant the credits you buy.
  • Usage information: how you use the website, such as pages viewed, posts viewed, likes and downloads on public posts, to operate and improve the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the MovAi service and your account.
  • To manage your credits, packages and purchases.
  • To show public posts you choose to publish, including your display name, and to rank them by views.
  • To communicate with you about your account, purchases and service updates.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only as needed to run the service:

  • Google Sign-In: authentication is provided by Google. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • AI generation providers: your prompts are sent to the generation provider you select (such as Magic Hour, NovAI, MusicAPI or Suno) so they can generate the content you request.
  • Public posts: anything you choose to publish as Public is visible to everyone who visits MovAi, including guests. Anything you keep Private is visible only to you.

4. Data storage and security

Your account data is stored on our server. Generated content you keep in your History is stored in your own browser. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You may choose to post content as Public or Private.
  • You may sign out at any time.
  • You may ask us to delete your account and personal data by contacting us at the email below. Deleting your account also removes your public posts.

6. Children's privacy

MovAi is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use MovAi or provide personal information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do, and continued use of MovAi after a change means you accept the updated policy.
