About MovAi

What is MovAi?

MovAi is a website where you can create images, videos, 3D models and songs with AI — all in one place. Sign in with your Google account, get free credits as a new user, and start creating.

What you can create

Images — AI images from a text prompt.
Videos — short AI videos from a prompt or a story.
3D Design — turn an image or text into a 3D model.
Songs — AI songs with your own title, lyrics, style and voice.

Public

Share your creations on the Public feed, where everyone can view, like and listen. The most-viewed creations rank at the top.

Credits & Packages

New users get free credits on their first Google sign-in — 250 Image.Video Credit and 24 Song Credit. More credits are available through packages, approved by the admin.
